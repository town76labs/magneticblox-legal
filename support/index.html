Subscriptions & refunds

MagneticBlox Pro subscriptions are billed by Apple App Store or Google Play. You can manage or cancel a subscription any time from your app store account settings.

Refund requests are handled by Apple or Google according to their policies. We’re happy to help you with the process — just email us.

Frequently asked questions

Does MagneticBlox work offline? Yes. All models and step recipes are bundled with the app.

Is there a parent gate? Yes. Purchase-related areas show a simple parent gate to keep kids out of subscription flows.

Do you collect personal data? No accounts, no ads, no tracking SDKs. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What devices are supported? iOS 15+ and Android 8+ (API 26+).
